Alias-ka

Privacy Policy — Last updated: June 29, 2026

No data collected

Overview

Alias-ka is an offline, local-multiplayer word game. It is designed to work entirely without an internet connection and without any servers. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information about you.

Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, process, or share:

  • Names, email addresses, or any account information
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Usage analytics or crash reports
  • Any data transmitted over the internet

Local Network (Bluetooth & Wi-Fi)

Alias-ka uses Android's Nearby Connections API to discover and connect nearby devices on the same local network. This communication is:

  • Strictly peer-to-peer between devices in the same room
  • Never routed through any external server or the internet
  • Limited to game data (word lists, scores, game events) for the duration of a game session
  • Not stored or logged after the session ends

Data Stored on Your Device

Alias-ka stores the following data locally on your device only:

  • Word sets — bundled word lists and any CSV files you choose to import
  • App preferences — language selection, round duration, target score

This data never leaves your device except to other devices you explicitly connect to during a game.

Permissions

The app requests the following Android permissions, used solely to enable local multiplayer:

  • Bluetooth — to discover and connect nearby devices
  • Wi-Fi / Nearby Wi-Fi Devices — to discover and connect nearby devices
  • Location (approximate) — required by Android for Bluetooth/Wi-Fi discovery; we do not read or store your location

Children's Privacy

Alias-ka does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. Because no data is collected at all, the app is safe for all ages.

Changes to This Policy

If we ever change this policy, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised date. Because we collect no data, any changes are expected to be minor clarifications only.
